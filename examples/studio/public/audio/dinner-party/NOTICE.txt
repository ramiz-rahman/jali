NOTICE: these files are modified.

p05.mp3, p06.mp3, p07.mp3 and p08.mp3 are modified from the DiPCo data (DiPCo - Dinner Party Corpus,
Amazon; https://zenodo.org/record/8122551). They are an excerpt of session S02 (18.50 to 146.00 s),
close-talk microphones P05 to P08: high-passed at 70 Hz, gated outside each wearer's own utterances
(-14 dB), resampled from 16 to 32 kHz, levelled, faded and encoded to MP3. The transcript in
src/sessions/data/dinner-party.json is from S02.json, verbatim, with word times added by forced alignment.
