One store is one workspace. Two stores on one page share nothing, except three things that belong to the page: the address, the browser's storage and the keyboard. Give each workspace its own share of those, and they work side by side.

## Its own name in the DOM

`id` prefixes the workspace's element ids (`orders-pane-…` and `notes-pane-…`, and its dialogs' ids) and its saved keys. The default is `"jali"`, so give each workspace its own. Focus, dragging and F6 already look only inside their own workspace's root.

## Its own params in the address

`createUrlSync({ prefix: "n." })` writes the notes workspace's params as `n.l`, `n.zoom` and `n.peek`. The orders workspace keeps the plain names. Each reads and writes only its own, and every other param is left alone, so one link reopens both.

A workspace that must not touch the address at all, such as one in a dialog, can use `createUrlSync({ location: memoryLocation() })`, or no `url` at all.

## Its own storage

A store saves its settings and pane state to `localStorage` under its `id`: `orders:settings`, `notes:pane-state`. Each URL sync keeps its own keys under its `prefix`. So two workspaces with their own ids and prefixes never overwrite each other. Pass `storage` to either for somewhere else, such as `browserStorage("my-app")` or `memoryStorage()`.

## Its own keys and start-up

`startUi(store, { root: store.dom.root })` sets a workspace up for the life of the page, scoped to it:

- **Keys:** it takes only keys from inside its root element and its dialogs, plus keys pressed with nothing focused, when that workspace was the last one used. So F6 in the notes moves between notes panes, and ⌘K then ⌘M maximises a notes pane. The palette renders outside the root, but it is the workspace's own, so the chord finishes there.
- **Back and Forward:** each workspace reads its own params again.
- **Saving** settings and pane state, and the browser's prompt when the page closes with unsaved work.

`store.dom.root` returns the element `<Jali>` renders, or the document before that.

**Theme:** `startUi` writes the theme setting to `data-theme` on `<html>`. With two workspaces, let one do it and pass `theme: false` to the other, as the sample does. Pass `theme: false` to both when your app runs its own theme; Jali then follows the system's dark mode, or your `.dark` and `[data-theme]` scopes.

For a workspace in another window or a same-origin iframe, call `installKeyboard(store, { root, target })` yourself. It returns a function that removes its listeners, as `startUi` does.

## Focus

Mounting a workspace never takes the page's focus. A page with three workspaces, or one below the fold, keeps its place. A workspace moves focus into its panes once the person is in it: when they click or tab into it, or run one of its commands.

## Its own look

To give one workspace its own accent, set `--jali-accent` on an element around its `<Jali>`. See [Theming](/guides/theming).
