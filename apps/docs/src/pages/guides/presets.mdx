A preset is a named layout: "Orders", "Orders and detail", "Customer desk". The workspace opens on one, ⌘K lists them all, and the person can switch at any time. Your app can switch too.

## A layout is a tree

A [layout](/get-started/key-concepts) is plain data: a tree of rows, columns, tab stacks and panes.

- A **pane** shows one ref. Its `id` is its name in the tree and in links; keep ids unique within a layout.
- A **row** puts its children side by side; a **column** stacks them top to bottom.
- A **stack** is a group of tabs. `active` names the tab shown; `origin` is `"user"`.
- Every node has a `size`. `{ weight: 1 }` shares the room with its siblings by weight. `{ px: 480 }` asks for a fixed length.

A pane opened from a list can follow it. Give it `linkedTo` (the list's pane id) and `follows: true`, and it shows whatever the list's cursor is on.

## Give them to the store

<Code code={storeCode} filename="src/workspace.ts" />

- `presets` is the list, in menu order. ⌘K shows each as "Layout: *label*".
- `preset` is the one to open on. Without it, the first. A link with a layout in it wins over both: see [Keep the layout in the URL](/guides/url-and-routers).
- `store.actions.preset(id)` switches. If a pane that would go has unsaved work, the person is asked first.
- `store.actions.resetLayout()` rebuilds the preset in use. ⌘K "Reset layout" does the same.

Switching to a preset is a new step in the browser's history, so Back returns to the layout before it.
